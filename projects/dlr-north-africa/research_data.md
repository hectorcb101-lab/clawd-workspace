# 🇲🇦 MOROCCO

## Central Bank: Bank Al-Maghrib (BAM)
- **Website:** www.bkam.ma
- **HQ:** Rabat
- **Currency:** Moroccan Dirham (MAD)
- **Exchange regime:** Managed float, ±5% band around central rate (60% EUR / 40% USD basket)

### Key Personnel (Org Chart)
- **Governor:** Abdellatif Jouahri (in office since 2003 — longest-serving governor)
- **Director General:** Abderrahim Bouazza
- **Board Members:**
  - Fathallah Oualalou
  - Mustapha Moussaoui
  - Larbi Jaïdi
  - Najat El Mekkaoui
  - Mohammed Daïri
  - Mouna Cherkaoui
  - Mohammed Tarik Bchir
  - Plus: Director of Treasury & External Finance (Finance Ministry rep)
- **Audit Committee:** Mouna Cherkaoui, Fathallah Oualalou, Mustapha Moussaoui (Chair)
- **Dar As-Sikkah (State Printing Works):** Located in Rabat, operational since 1987. Morocco's domestic banknote printer.

### Banknote Denominations (Current Series — 2012/2013 + 2023 new series)
| Denomination | Colour | Size (mm) | Substrate | Printer | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 MAD | Purple/blue | 138×69 | Paper | Dar As-Sikkah | King Mohammed VI portrait |
| 50 MAD | Green | 147×70 | Paper | Dar As-Sikkah | King Mohammed VI, Bab Essebaa gate |
| 100 MAD | Brown | 153×72 | Paper | Dar As-Sikkah | Three Kings (new 2023 issue) |
| 200 MAD | Blue | 159×74 | Paper | Dar As-Sikkah | King Mohammed VI |

**2023 New Series:** Unveiled 24 November 2023 — new family of banknotes and coins. Includes new 100 MAD note.

**Commemorative:** 25 MAD (2012) — 25th anniversary of Dar As-Sikkah, printed on Landqart Durasafe® hybrid substrate.

### Key Data
- **Population:** ~37.5 million (2024)
- **GDP:** ~$150 billion (2024)
- **Inflation:** ~0.2-2%
- **Cash in circulation:** BAM reports available via annual report (2024 published)
- **Dirham not freely convertible** on international markets
- **Printer:** Dar As-Sikkah (100% domestic printing since 1987)
- **De La Rue historical involvement:** De La Rue printed Moroccan banknotes BEFORE Dar As-Sikkah was established. Morocco moved to full domestic printing in 1987.

### Opportunities for DLR
- **Substrate supply:** Dar As-Sikkah may source paper/substrate externally
- **Security features:** Thread, holograms, security inks — procurement possible
- **Technology/equipment:** Printing press maintenance, upgrades
- **Consultancy:** New series design support
- **NOTE:** Morocco is largely self-sufficient via Dar As-Sikkah — primary opportunity is in components/substrate rather than finished banknotes

# 🇩🇿 ALGERIA

## Central Bank: Banque d'Algérie (Bank of Algeria)
- **Website:** www.bank-of-algeria.dz
- **HQ:** Algiers
- **Currency:** Algerian Dinar (DZD)
- **Exchange regime:** Managed float (significant parallel market — official ~134 DZD/USD vs parallel ~238 DZD/USD)

### Key Personnel (Org Chart)
- **Governor:** Mouatassem Boudiaf (appointed January 2026, replacing Salah Eddine Taleb who was dismissed)
  - *Note: Taleb was in office May 2022 – January 2026*
  - *Previous: Rosthom Fadli (2020-2022), Aïmene Benabderrahmane (2019-2020)*
- **Vice-Governor:** TBC (frequent changes in Algerian central bank leadership)
- **Key fact:** ~50% of Algeria's currency exists outside the banking system — massive informal economy

**Frequently used:** 500, 1000, 2000 DA

### Key Data
- **Population:** ~45.6 million (2024)
- **GDP:** ~$240 billion (2024)
- **Inflation:** ~4% (2024)
- **Foreign reserves:** $64.63 billion (2023)
- **Cash in circulation:** Very high — informal economy drives massive cash demand
- **Printer:** Algeria has domestic printing capability (Bank of Algeria printing works), but historically sourced from Banque de France and other external printers
- **Recent new issues:** 2018 (500 DA, 1000 DA new designs), 2020-2022 (2000 DA new designs)

### Opportunities for DLR
- **MAJOR OPPORTUNITY:** Algeria prints domestically but sources components externally
- **New series activity:** Multiple new denominations issued 2018-2022 — active modernisation programme
- **High cash demand:** Large informal economy, 50%+ currency outside banking = high volume requirements
- **Security upgrades:** Holographic strips added to 1998 series — appetite for modern security
- **Substrate supply:** Paper/polymer substrate procurement
- **Complete banknote supply:** If domestic capacity insufficient for volume

# 🇹🇳 TUNISIA

## Central Bank: Banque Centrale de Tunisie (BCT)
- **Website:** www.bct.gov.tn
- **HQ:** Tunis
- **Currency:** Tunisian Dinar (TND)
- **Exchange regime:** Managed float (closed currency — cannot be exported/imported)

### Key Personnel (Org Chart)
- **Governor:** Fethi Zouhair Nouri (appointed 15 February 2024)
  - Background: Professor of economics, extensive academic career since 1982, served in various economic/financial institutions 1990-2016
  - Previous: Marouane El Abassi (2018-2024), Chedly Ayari (2013-2018)
- **Vice-Governor:** TBC (appointed by presidential decree for 6-year term)
- **Board of Directors:** Governor (chair) + Vice-Governor + up to 8 members appointed by President
- **Four Primary Directorates:**
  1. Monetary Policy
  2. Banking Supervision
  3. Systems & Means of Payment
  4. (Operations/Currency — exact name TBC)
  - Each headed by Director General reporting to Governor

### Banknote Denominations (Current Series — 2017-2022 upgraded series)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 TND | Green | 143×73 | 2022 | Slaheddine El Amami (agricultural engineer) | External | RAPID® HD thread |
| 10 TND | Blue/yellow | 148×73 | 2020 | Dr. Tawhida Ben Cheikh (first female doctor) | External | RAPID® HD thread |
| 20 TND | Red/orange | 153×76 | 2017 | Farhat Hached (trade unionist) | External | RAPID® thread |

**Key:** Complete series upgrade 2017-2022 using Crane Currency's RAPID® HD security thread across all denominations.

### Historical DLR Connection
- **De La Rue printed the 1972 Tunisia series** (P-66, P-67, P-68: ½, 1, and 5 Dinars)
- Historical relationship exists

### Current Printers
- **Crane Currency** — primary supplier for current series (RAPID® HD is Crane's proprietary technology)
- Banknotes printed on paper with protective varnish coat
- Previously: Various including De La Rue (1972), others

### Key Data
- **Population:** ~12.4 million (2024)
- **GDP:** ~$48 billion (2024)
- **Inflation:** ~7-8%
- **Currency restrictions:** Closed currency — cannot be exported or imported. All exchange must occur within Tunisia
- **Denominations:** 5, 10, 20, 50 TND only (coins for smaller values)
- **1 TND ≈ $0.32 USD** (as of mid-2024)
- **Credit rating:** Moody's Caa1 (junk), B3 range — high fiscal risk
- **Foreign reserves:** ~3.5 months of imports (Dec 2025)
- **Budget deficit:** ~6.0% GDP (2026 est)

### Opportunities for DLR
- **COMPETITIVE DISPLACEMENT:** Crane Currency is incumbent — DLR would need to offer superior value
- **Historical relationship:** DLR printed 1972 series — re-entry angle
- **Fiscal pressure:** Tunisia under severe fiscal strain — cost-competitive offering could win
- **Next series:** Current series completed 2022 — next upgrade cycle likely 2027-2030
- **Security features:** Could offer alternative to Crane's RAPID® thread
- **Substrate:** Paper with varnish — could propose polymer or enhanced substrate
