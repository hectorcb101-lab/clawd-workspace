Key Currency Intelligence

  • 2022 Redesign Crisis: CBN under Emefiele redesigned ₦200, ₦500, ₦1,000 — led to severe cash crisis, street protests, and Supreme Court intervention
  • De La Rue designed the 2022 redesign — engaged by NSPM/CBN for the work
  • Lower denominations (₦5-₦50): Polymer banknotes since 2007-2009 (CBN later said no more polymer — higher costs, environmental issues)
  • Higher denominations (₦100-₦1,000): Paper (cotton) substrate
  • eNaira: CBDC launched Oct 2021 — adoption remains limited
  • Massive cash economy: 230M+ population, significant unbanked population
  • Naira at ~₦1,600/USD (collapsed from ₦460 in 2023 after float)
  • Bank recapitalisation deadline: 31 March 2026 — ₦500B for international banks

DLR Opportunity Assessment

🔥 HIGHEST PRIORITY — Established Relationship:
  • 1% ownership in NSPM — existing structural relationship
  • Original currency designer — designed 2022 redesign, deep institutional knowledge
  • 230M+ population — Africa's largest banknote market by volume
  • Massive demand: High inflation + large cash economy = constant need for fresh notes
  • New governor (2023): Cardoso bringing transparency/reform agenda — opportunity for formal engagement
  • NSPM capacity limitations: Domestic printer needs DLR support for redesigns and capacity overflow
  • Substrate, security features, design services: Ongoing pipeline
  • ₦5,000 denomination: Under consideration since 2012 — would require new design work
Challenges:
  • 2023 currency crisis caused political backlash — sensitivity around redesigns
  • CBN abandoned polymer citing costs/environmental concerns
  • EFCC investigation into Emefiele era — governance scrutiny
  • FX volatility may affect procurement budgets

Key Currency Intelligence

  • De La Rue is the INCUMBENT SUPPLIER — prints all Seychelles banknotes

DLR Opportunity Assessment

✅ EXISTING CLIENT — Relationship Management:

  • Security feature upgrades: New generation holograms, threads
Considerations:
  • Very small market (~100K population, 4 denominations) — low volume
  • Limited revenue opportunity per contract cycle
  • But: high-value reference client, stable relationship, good margins on small runs
