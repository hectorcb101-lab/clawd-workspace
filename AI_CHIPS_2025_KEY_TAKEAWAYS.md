## 🏆 MAJOR PLAYER ROADMAPS

### NVIDIA: Blackwell → Rubin → Feynman

**Timeline:**
- 2025 Q4: Blackwell Ultra
- **2026: Rubin Platform (announced Jan 2026)**
  - 6 chips: GPU, CPU, NVLink 6, ConnectX-9, BlueField-4, Spectrum-6
  - 10x reduction in inference token cost vs Blackwell
  - 4x fewer GPUs needed for training MoE models
- 2027: Rubin Ultra
- Beyond: Feynman

**Strategy shift:**
- Annual cadence (down from 2-year)
- Rack-scale platform (not just chips)
- Inference optimization (responding to DeepSeek/efficiency narrative)

### AMD: CDNA 4 (MI350) → CDNA 5 (MI400) → MI500

**Timeline:**
- H2 2025: MI350 series (CDNA 4, 3nm, 288GB HBM3E)
- **2026: MI400 series (CDNA 5)**
  - Three SKUs: MI430X, MI440X, MI455X
  - 432GB HBM4 memory (vs 288GB)
  - 19.6 TB/s bandwidth (vs 8 TB/s)
  - 40 FP4 / 20 FP8 PFLOPs
- Beyond 2026: MI500 previewed (CDNA Next)

**Helios Platform:** Rack-scale system competing with NVIDIA Rubin

**Impact:** Forcing NVIDIA to boost Rubin performance (per Tom's Hardware)

### Intel: Gaudi 3 → Jaguar Shores (Falcon Shores CANCELLED)

**Timeline:**
- 2025: Gaudi 3 (missed AI wave, inventory problems)
- **CANCELLED: Falcon Shores** (was due late 2025)
  - Reason: Pivot to rack-scale vs standalone chip
- **2026: Jaguar Shores**
  - Rack-scale solution
  - Leap-frog strategy after Falcon cancellation

**Challenges:**
- Software gap (Gaudi repo archived Feb 2025)
- Execution issues (Falcon cancellation = credibility hit)
- CUDA lock-in remains barrier

## 🔮 2026 PREDICTIONS

### What to watch:

**1. M&A Wave (HIGH PROBABILITY)**
- Cerebras = prime target (hyperscaler or chip giant)
- SambaNova = strategic for sovereign AI angle
- d-Matrix, Tachyum, others "in play"

**2. Platform Wars Intensify**
- NVIDIA Rubin vs AMD Helios vs Intel Jaguar Shores
- Chip-only startups squeezed out
- Software ecosystems decide winners

**3. Inference/Training Market Split**
- Specialized inference chips validated (Groq acquisition)
- FP4/FP8 becoming standard
- Different economics than training

**4. Memory Becomes Bottleneck**
- AMD's HBM4 (432GB) first-mover advantage
- NVIDIA's 10x inference efficiency = memory optimization
- HBM supply chain (SK Hynix/Samsung) = chokepoint

**5. China Ecosystem Diverges**
- Separate standards (DeepSeek FP8)
- Domestic market protection (Huawei tripling output)
- Different performance metrics (efficiency vs raw compute)

### Critical questions:

1. **Does NVIDIA's Groq buy trigger consolidation wave?** → YES
2. **Can AMD sustain "tens of billions" AI revenue?** → Depends on MI400 execution
3. **Will Intel's Jaguar Shores salvage AI strategy?** → Make-or-break 2026
4. **Does sovereign AI create viable third market?** → SambaNova's 4 customers = proof
5. **How fast can China close 1-2 year gap?** → Production ramp suggests <18 months
