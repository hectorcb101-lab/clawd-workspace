📚 **LEARN: VIX (Volatility Index)**

The VIX measures expected volatility in the S&P 500 over the next 30 days, derived from options prices. Often called the 'fear index' because it spikes when investors expect market turbulence.

*Today:* VIX is at 20.2, up 3.1% today - showing rising market uncertainty
