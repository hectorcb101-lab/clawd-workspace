## 💡 Pro Tips for Maximum Impact

### **First 3 Events (Start Here):**
1. **Data & AI London (Tue 17 Feb)** — Largest community, easy to blend in
2. **Tech Startups in the Pub (Fri 20 Feb)** — Relaxed, social, no pressure
3. **London Agentic AI Meet-Up (Wed 25 Feb)** — Technical depth, cutting-edge

### **Preparation Checklist:**
- [ ] Update LinkedIn with "MSc AI @ QMUL" headline
- [ ] Create LinkedIn QR code for easy connection sharing
- [ ] Prepare 30-second intro: "I'm Finn, doing an MSc in AI at Queen Mary while building AI systems for sales and marketing. Currently interested in [specific topic]."
- [ ] Set Google Calendar reminders for each event
- [ ] Join Meetup.com and RSVP to lock in your spot
- [ ] Check venue locations the day before (most in Central/East London)

### **At Each Event:**
- **Goal:** Connect with 3-5 people meaningfully (not 50 superficially)
- **Approach:** Find people standing alone or in small groups
- **Questions to ask:** "What brought you here?" "What are you working on?" "What's the most interesting thing you've learned about [topic] recently?"
- **Follow up within 24-48 hours:** LinkedIn connection with personal note referencing your conversation

### **Don't Attend If:**
- You're exhausted (quality > quantity)
- You have a major deadline the next day
- The topic doesn't genuinely interest you (authenticity matters)

### **Weekly Rhythm:**
- **1-2 evening events** (weekday)
- **1 weekend event** (optional, often lower pressure)
- **1 online event** (flexible around schedule)
- = **2-4 events per week maximum** (sustainable pace)
