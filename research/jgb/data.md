## 7. CORRELATION: JGB 10-Year vs US 10-Year Treasury

### Current Levels (February 1, 2026)

| Bond | Yield | Source |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Japan 10Y JGB** | 2.25% | Trading Economics |
| **US 10Y Treasury** | 4.24% | Trading Economics |
| **Spread (US - Japan)** | **1.99%** (199 basis points) | Calculated |

### Historical Correlation Patterns

**General Observations:**
- **Positive Correlation:** JGB and UST yields tend to move in the same direction over long periods
- **Correlation Strength:** Moderate to strong (varies 0.5-0.8 depending on time period)
- **Spread:** US yields typically 100-300 bps higher than JGB yields
- **Divergence Periods:** Correlation weakens during BOJ policy shifts or US-specific events

**Recent Trends:**
- Both yields rising as central banks normalize policy
- Spread has widened as Fed kept rates higher for longer
- JGB yields rising faster in percentage terms (from near-zero base)

**Factors Affecting Correlation:**
1. Global economic growth expectations
2. Central bank policy divergence/convergence
3. Inflation differentials
4. Safe-haven flows during risk events
5. Currency hedging costs (JPY/USD)

**Note:** Detailed correlation statistics require econometric analysis of historical time series data.
